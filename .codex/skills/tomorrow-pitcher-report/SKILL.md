# tomorrow-pitcher Report

## Report Rules

Keep reports short and action-oriented. Focus on what changed, why it matters, and what should happen next.

## Include

- Completed work.
- Important decisions.
- Changed files or areas.
- Verification performed.
- Blockers or risks.
- Next recommended tasks.
- Decisions recorded or still needing decision records.

## Project Lens

Always call out when relevant:

- MVP scope impact.
- Data trust/source freshness.
- Supabase/service-role safety.
- KST/date handling.
- Official/system/fan/actual starter separation.
- Crawler readiness.

## Output Shape

Use:

- `Done`
- `Decisions`
- `Verification`
- `Risks`
- `Next`
